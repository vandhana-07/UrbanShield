## 📝 Word-for-Word Presenter Script

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Problem (Hook & Context)
*(Presenter 1 points to the top overview)*

> *"Good afternoon, judges. Climate volatility is creating severe urban cloudbursts and tidal flooding in our metropolitan centers. Today, municipal emergency commanders are flooded with raw sensor numbers across rainfall, drainage, and soil moisture—but lack predictive clarity and automated dispatch optimization.
> 
> Meet **UrbanShield**—an autonomous municipal resilience and resource optimization engine that turns sensor telemetry into prioritized, explainable operational defense."*

---

### [0:25 – 0:50] The 6-Stage Autonomous Pipeline
*(Presenter 2 gestures to the 6-stage pipeline stepper at the top of the dashboard)*

> *"UrbanShield connects raw data to field deployment through an end-to-end 6-stage pipeline:
> 1. **SENSE:** Continuously ingests 6 live environmental sensor feeds across all city sectors.
> 2. **PREDICT:** Evaluates real-time threat probabilities using a trained Random Forest model.
> 3. **PRIORITIZE:** Ranks sectors by consequence weighting against population density and critical infrastructure.
> 4. **OPTIMIZE:** Solves pump and crew resource allocation using Google OR-Tools.
> 5. **RECOMMEND:** Generates plain-language explainable action briefs for field commanders.
> 6. **SIMULATE:** Dynamically recalculates scenarios under severe weather stress."*

---

### [0:50 – 1:20] Live Zone Drill-Down: Sector Z-01
*(Presenter 2 clicks Zone Z-01 on the interactive Folium map)*

> *"Looking at our City Overview, UrbanShield immediately flags **Zone Z-01: South Lowland Basin** as our **#1 Most Urgent Threat** with an **88% Critical Flood Probability**.
> 
> When I click Z-01 on the map, notice how the entire decision pipeline synchronizes instantly:
> - **Environmental Driver:** Active monsoon precipitation at **68.5 mm/hr** combined with severely choked **32% drainage capacity**.
> - **Protected Consequence:** Safeguards **Metro General Hospital** and **Substation Beta** serving **125,000 residents**.
> - **Optimized Dispatch:** OR-Tools deployed **4 Heavy Pumps and 2 Rapid Response Crews** to clear hospital access culverts and prevent a trauma center blackout."*

---

### [1:20 – 1:55] What-If Simulation Sandbox
*(Presenter 2 switches to the '🔄 What-If Simulation Sandbox' tab and clicks '🌊 Coastal Typhoon & Surge')*

> *"Now, what if conditions deteriorate? Let's open our What-If Sandbox.
> 
> With one click on **'Coastal Typhoon & Surge'**:
> - The multi-layer pipeline recalculates live across all 6 sectors.
> - City-wide average risk surges to **92%**.
> - Total pump demand jumps from 14 to **23 heavy pumps**.
> - UrbanShield immediately highlights an equipment deficit of **🚨 9 Pumps**, alerting command to activate regional mutual-aid protocols before floodwaters overwhelm the coastal sea wall.
> 
> *(Presenter 2 clicks '☀️ Nominal Baseline')*
> And when weather normalizes, clicking **'Nominal Baseline'** immediately rebalances resources to **0 equipment deficit**."*

---

### [1:55 – 2:15] Resilience & Closing
*(Presenter 1 concludes)*

> *"UrbanShield operates on a resilient 3-tier fallback architecture: connecting seamlessly to our live REST API, local SQLite database, or calibrated offline fallback with sub-5 millisecond response times.
> 
> UrbanShield turns chaotic municipal telemetry into optimal urban defense in under a second. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"*
